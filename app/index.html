Quick FAQ

Q: Can I earn cashback and claim airdrops?

A: Yes! They're independent. Trade for cashback and hold $PUMP for airdrop tiers — both use the same wallet.

Q: What if I use an exchange wallet?

A: Exchange wallets (Binance,bybit, Coinbase) are NOT eligible. Use self-custody wallets only(phantom,solflare,bacckpack).

Q: How often is cashback calculated?

A: Real-time. Every transaction instantly adds to your claimable balance in SOL.

Q: Are airdrops taxable?

A: Check local laws. We provide full transaction history for tax reporting.

Q: How is my airdrop allocation calculated?

A: Based on a random snapshot of your $PUMP holdings × your tier multiplier (1x–5x).

Q: What happens to unclaimed airdrops?

A: Unclaimed rewards after 20 days are redistributed to active claimers in the next round.

Q: Do I need SOL for gas?

A: Yes — keep ~0.002 SOL in your wallet for the claim transaction. Most rewards cost less than 0.001 SOL in gas.

Q: Can I use multiple wallets?

A: Yes, but each wallet must independently meet volume and holding requirements. Rewards are per-wallet.
